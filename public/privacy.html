Privacy

Flowtape is designed to keep your music, projects, and ideas on your own device.

Controller

For the website and Flowtape desktop app, the controller is Sandro Ieva, Arnimstrasse 13, 23566 Lübeck, Germany. For privacy questions, contact support@i7os.com.

The Flowtape app

Flowtape is local-first. Audio files, imported sounds, projects, and app settings are stored on the computer on which you use the app. Flowtape does not provide user accounts, cloud synchronisation, or built-in analytics at this time.

You decide which audio files you import. The app copies imported material into its local app storage so that it remains available even if the original source file is moved or removed. This information is not sent to us by the app.

Website hosting and downloads

This website is hosted through Vercel. When you visit, Vercel may process technical connection data, such as IP address, browser information, date and time, and requested pages, to deliver and secure the site. Software downloads are provided through GitHub Releases. Clicking a download link transfers you to GitHub, which processes data under its own privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

The Flowtape website does not currently use advertising cookies or visitor analytics. We do not sell personal data.

Your rights
